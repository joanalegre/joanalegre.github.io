Job Market Paper

• Choosing What to Calibrate and What to Estimate in Structural Models

[Paper]
[Abstract]

Structural models often fix some parameters and estimate the rest, but the calibration-estimation partition is usually chosen by convention. This paper treats that choice as an econometric partition-selection problem. For each admissible partition, I construct a scalar sensitivity statistic measuring the local response of a target object to perturbations of the calibrated parameters. The selected partition minimizes this statistic and therefore minimizes worst-case local bias from calibration errors. The procedure requires only local derivatives, avoids repeated re-estimation, and applies to a broad class of structural models.
